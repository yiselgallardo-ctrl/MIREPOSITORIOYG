BUSINESS MANAGEMENT

Behind the Scenes of Metro Line 3

For our Business Management class, my classmates and I studied Panama Metro’s Line 3. We arranged a visit to the construction site, met engineers, and interviewed architects and workers to learn how a project of this scale is organized.

What I Learned

Talking with the people on site helped me understand what happens behind the scenes of a large project. I saw how teams with different responsibilities need to plan, communicate, and coordinate their work. The interviews also helped me practice asking clear questions and listening to people with different roles.

What I Take With Me

These experiences taught me to speak with more confidence, listen to people with different perspectives, and see what we can accomplish when we work together.
