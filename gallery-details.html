HYDRONEXIS

Over three years, I went from taking part in my first Hackathon to presenting a home hydroponics prototype with my team.

2024 · Hackathon

Our project began with a challenge to develop an idea related to agriculture. During the Hackathon, my team came up with the concept for HYDRONEXIS: a hydroponic system that would help people grow plants at home.

2025 · Expo Logros

At Expo Logros, we developed the idea further. We worked on the project’s visual identity, website, and presentation. Our team’s speakers then presented the concept at an internal exhibition at our ¡Supérate! center.

2026 · EXPOTECH

At EXPOTECH, we competed with teams from other ¡Supérate! centers. We brought our hydroponic prototype and presented it to an audience and a panel of judges who evaluated our project. We showed how our system could help people grow plants at home.

My Role as a Speaker

I represented our team on stage alongside a teammate. Presenting HYDRONEXIS taught me how to explain a technical idea clearly, move more naturally on stage, and speak with greater confidence in front of an audience.

Community & Sustainability

Volunteering with TECHO

TECHO Panamá works with communities and volunteers to address housing needs, including building emergency homes. During our volunteer day, my teammates and I helped assemble parts of a home for a family. It was hard work, and seeing what we could do together for that family made it one of my most rewarding experiences.

“What stayed with me was knowing that a day of teamwork could help give a family a place to call home.”

Recycling with Hogar EcoEduca

Hogar EcoEduca supports recycling education and participates in Panama City’s Basura Cero program. During my three years at ¡Supérate!, my classmates and I collected and delivered different recyclable materials as our contribution.
